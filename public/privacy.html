Zero-Tracking & On-Device Storage Philosophy

At Viflo (https://viflo.app), we believe your travel history, passport validity details, visa stamps, and Schengen stay counters belong exclusively to you. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track your location, and we display no third-party advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

Viflo operates on a strict principle of data minimization:

  • Local Travel Records: Country entry/exit dates, visa expirations, and passport numbers are stored locally within your device's encrypted app sandbox.
  • Optional Cloud Backups: If you explicitly enable encrypted sync, your logs are encrypted end-to-end (AES-256) and can be deleted at any time.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Fully anonymized crash logs aggregated by Apple App Store and Google Play are used exclusively to fix app stability issues.

2. Device Permissions

The app requests device permissions strictly when needed:

  • Notifications: To trigger local expiry countdowns for Schengen limits and passport renewals.
  • Camera (Optional): For on-device scanning of physical visa stickers. Scans are processed locally without cloud transfer.
  • Files / Storage: Solely to export generated PDF and CSV visa reports for embassies.

3. Third-Party Services & Subscriptions

Payment transactions for Viflo Pro are processed entirely by Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing. Viflo never handles, receives, or stores payment card information.

4. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

You retain 100% ownership of your data:

  • Export: Download your entire travel history as standard JSON or CSV files anytime.
  • Erasure: Permanently wipe all data with a single tap in the app's settings.
